# squared.today

_last updated Oct 26, 2026, 04:19 UTC_

A quiet, minimal app where I paint one square per day with a color for how I spent that day. I'm the only user. This is version one — build exactly what's here and nothing more.

## Platform

- Mobile-first responsive web app (PWA), not a native app.
- Must install to an iPhone home screen.
- Used mostly on phone; desktop should work too.

## Stack

- React + Vite + TypeScript
- Supabase for database and auth
- Deployed to Vercel
- GitHub repo: `asahinanomi/squared`

## The painting interaction (the most important part)

- A painting palette holding three colors. It should read and behave like a real paint palette, not generic buttons.
- Tapping a color loads the brush with it. The brush stays loaded until a different color is picked.
- A square is painted by dragging across it with a finger (or mouse on desktop). The color follows the stroke, like real paint on paper.
- One fixed medium brush size — no thickness picker. About two swipes should fill a square.
- The brush can paint the full square. **Tapping a square to fill it instantly is NOT acceptable. Painting is the point.**
- Any past or present day can be painted at any time (backfilling is fine). Future days can't be painted — you can't reflect on a day that hasn't happened yet. (Changed from "no date restrictions" during v1 review.)
- Painting with a clean brush makes the wells hop one after another (green, grey, red) as a quiet "pick a color" cue.
- The palette itself must show which color is loaded, since a finger covers the square while painting: each well is a shallow bowl of paint; dipping stirs that well (light and dark swirls), spills drops over its rim onto the board, and fills the brush head with that color. The palette is drawn after the owner's reference illustration: cream board, three paint bowls, dark brush with a metal band resting in the thumb notch.

## The three colors

- **Green** — I lived consciously. Good or bad, I spent the day meaningfully.
- **Grey** — I wasn't conscious. The day just slipped by.
- **Red** — I was conscious, but I didn't spend the day meaningfully, and I wish I'd chosen differently.

Shades (from the phase 2 design): green `#6E9B7B`, grey `#8E8E87`, red `#C06A62` — the paint itself, on the day's canvas. Every square in the overviews (month, list, year and life bands, a life in days) uses one softer shade instead (`#A7C4B0`, `#AEAEA6`, `#D9A8A3`), however late it was painted (its line says when), so a full month reads calm. A framed day keeps the full paint there, framed in `#53735C` (or filled with it where squares are too small for a frame, and as its own strip at the bottom of green in the bands), so it pops among the soft ones.

In the app the meanings read, in the brand story's words: **green** "yes, I spent it on what matters." · **red** "no, I wish I'd spent it differently." · **grey** "I don't know… I wasn't paying attention." Short labels, everywhere space is tight (the word in the square, legends, filters): **worth it** · **regret** · **a blur** (renamed Oct 23, 2026 from mattered · empty · slipped: they sound like the honest answer to "how was your day?"; see the brand story v3). In counts: "3 worth it", "2 regrets", "1 blur"; just painted: "your 3rd day worth it this month". The question stays "did I spend Today meaningfully?". The data keeps its names (`green`, `red`, `grey`; the compass's `meaningful` and `wasted`). Always listed green, red, grey. They show in the "meaningful" sheet, and as a quiet grey line (led by a small dot of that paint) under the palette each time a color is dipped (it fades after a few seconds, or as soon as painting starts).

## The why note

- Once a square is filled, a small gentle bubble appears with "because…" (asked "why?" until Oct 23, 2026: an open sentence you finish, gentler than a question). The note can be as long as needed, with paragraph breaks (up to 10,000 characters). (Was a single-line input; changed during v1 review.)
- Notes keep a history: a day can hold several notes, each stamped with when it was written (stored in a `notes` table, migration 0004). They show under the palette, all open and in full, oldest → newest, each stamped like "Wed, Oct 14, 2026 · 4:52 pm". The latest note always shows; a "3 notes … show older / hide older" row tucks the older ones away (remembered on that device): plain words, ink-soft, with a small chevron (down while there's more to open, up to tuck it away), and a thin line close under the row, its header's rule, so the notes clearly start there (it was a keyline pill; it competed).
- Tap a note to open it: **update** (saves the change; its time becomes now) or **delete** (a grey button; an in-app "Delete this note? It can't be undone." alert confirms). Swiping a note left also reveals a grey **delete**, with the same alert. Tapping outside leaves it as it was. Below the last note, an **add new note** button always opens an empty box. Painting a day opens "because…" as a new note.
- Notes save instantly: the box closes (button: **done**) and the note shows at once while it saves in the background. If saving fails, the change is undone and the box reopens with the text and a quiet message. Each request gives up after 10s and retries once on a fresh connection (phones can wake the app on a dead one); the sign-in is renewed as soon as the app comes back on screen.
- Easy to dismiss — tapping outside closes it; skipping is fine. Never required, never nagging.
- Text only. No voice recording.

## Visual rules (strict)

- The only color anywhere in the app is in the painted squares and the palette. Everything else is black, white and grey.
- Large, very readable type. Generous spacing. Calm and quiet.
- No badges, streaks, counters, confetti, notifications or celebration of any kind. This is a reflection tool, not a productivity app.
- An unpainted day stays blank. No guilt messages, no broken-streak warnings.

Width and size:
- **One width for every main action**: 250px, left-aligned (centred only on onboarding and sign-in; narrower only on a narrower screen), 54px tall with 18px text: start painting, now paint today, send, close, every onboarding step. The white Google sign-in pill is the same 250px wide (50px tall).
- **Hugs its text**: actions inside a card or bubble (done / delete, at least 88px; add new note).
- **Equal halves**: a two-choice dialog (cancel | delete).
- Questions and titles never have a hard line break: they wrap where the screen ends.
- Tap targets are never smaller than 44px.

## Data

- A `days` table in Supabase: `date`, `color`, `note`, and a boolean for whether the square was painted on the day itself or backfilled later (set automatically).
- Painted squares persist and reload when the app is reopened.
- The question text ("did I spend Today meaningfully?", set by 0006) is stored as a single configurable value, not hardcoded in the UI.

## Auth

Supabase Auth with Google sign-in only. No Facebook, no email/password.

Zoom levels (week/month/year/lifetime views), painting history before this month, voice notes, AI features, sharing or public grids, Facebook login, brush thickness options, settings screens, onboarding flows, analytics.

### Feedback by email (one-time setup)

Each new note is also emailed to the maker; the table keeps them all either way.

1. **Resend** (resend.com, free): sign up with the address the emails should go to, and create an API key.
2. **Supabase → Edge Functions → Deploy a new function → Via editor**: name it `feedback-email`, paste `supabase/functions/feedback-email/index.ts`, deploy.
3. **Edge Functions → Secrets**: add `RESEND_API_KEY` (the key), `FEEDBACK_TO` (the address to email) and `FEEDBACK_SECRET` (any long random text). In the function's **Settings**, turn **Verify JWT** off — the secret is the check instead (projects on Supabase's newer API keys reject the old JWT anyway). Optional: `FEEDBACK_FROM`, once a domain is verified in Resend (until then it sends from Resend's test sender, which can only email the Resend account's own address).
4. **Integrations → Database Webhooks → Create** (enable webhooks first if asked): table `feedback`, event *Insert*, type *Supabase Edge Functions*, function `feedback-email`, and an HTTP header `x-feedback-secret` with the same text as `FEEDBACK_SECRET`.
5. Send yourself a note from the app to check.

The address lives only in Supabase secrets, never in the (public) code. The sign-in screen also carries a quiet "the idea" link under the Google button, for someone new; opened from there it has a "‹ sign in" back arrow and no link on to what matters.

- **What matters is one row per line** (`matters_entries`, migration 0017, which copied your lists in; 0018 removed the old `matters` table and its history): each line added, edited, deleted or moved saves on its own (what changed against what the account had), so edits from two devices add up instead of one list overwriting the other. - **The demo** (no account) keeps everything on the device.
- **Why a save failed is written down** (`src/lib/report.ts`, migration 0015): each failure becomes one line (what, error code and message, status, how long it took, online or not, how long since the app came back to the screen, the browser), kept on the phone first and sent to `client_errors` when it can be. ## Safety and stability (before beta, Oct 22, 2026)

- **Every deploy is checked first**: Vercel runs `npm run lint && npm test && npm run build` (`vercel.json` → buildCommand), so a failing test or lint error stops the deploy and the live site stays as it was. Tests (`npm test`, Vitest) live next to the code: `src/lib/saving.test.ts` checks what matters (add, reorder, edit, delete, two devices) and the save queue (a blip retried quietly; offline says "not saved"; "try again" saves).
- **A crash never shows a blank page** (`src/components/CrashScreen.tsx`): "Something went wrong. Your squares are safe in your account." with "reload"; what broke is written to `client_errors`, and so are errors outside a screen (scripts, promises nobody caught).
- **Security headers** (`vercel.json`): a content policy (scripts only from squared itself; fonts from Google Fonts; no framing by other sites), plus `X-Frame-Options`, `X-Content-Type-Options`, `Referrer-Policy`, `Permissions-Policy` and HSTS.
- **Every table is locked to its owner** (row-level security: each person reads and writes only their own rows). No secret keys in the app or the repo; the admin key lives only in the feedback email function.
- **Feedback is limited** to 5 messages per person per day (migration 0019, a check in the database): the sixth says "that's today's limit of five messages. thank you, send more tomorrow". No captcha (it would need a third-party script, and "get in touch" already needs a Google account).
- **Packages**: `npm audit` is clean; run it again about monthly.
- **Backups**: on Supabase Pro, daily backups are kept for 7 days (Dashboard → Database → Backups). Once a month, download one (or Dashboard → Database → Backups → download) and keep it somewhere private: a copy outside Supabase.
