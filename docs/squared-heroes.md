# squared's heroes

_last updated Oct 26, 2026_

What squared is best at. Come back to this whenever squared grows: every new thing should make a hero stronger, or it doesn't belong.

## The three heroes

1. **The brush: painting your day.** No typing, no mood faces: you paint. Physical, a little slow, honest. The moment people remember.
2. **The zoom out: a life in days.** Two pinches and today is one small square among every day you'll have. The urgency, felt, not told.
3. **The question: "am I spending Today meaningfully?"** The voice of squared. Not how you feel, but what you did with your life today.

**The supporting cast:** moments feed the brush; the paintings reward it.

## How it should feel

Minimal, super pleasant, easy. You feel drawn to it, not pushed. Calm, never techy.

## The test

Before adding anything, ask: which hero does this serve?

- The day points to the brush.
- The zoom points to your life.
- My days points to what you've painted.

If it serves none, it goes (stats, counts, extra rows). If it competes with a hero, it goes.

## Decided so far

- **The paint is gouache:** dry-brush streaks, opaque, matte, one flat coat. Not watercolour (colours would blur into each other).
- **The day's word** ("worth it", "regret", "a blur") signs the lower right corner, like an artist's signature.
- **Never:** streaks, badges, scores, feeds, dashboards of numbers.
