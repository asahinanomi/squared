# squared's heroes

_last updated Oct 31, 2026_

What squared is best at. Come back to this whenever squared grows: every new thing should make a hero stronger, or it doesn't belong.

## The three heroes

1. **The brush: painting your day.** No typing, no mood faces: you paint. Physical, a little slow, honest. The moment people remember.
2. **Seeing your life.** The same days, looked at in three ways (revisited Oct 31, 2026: it began as "the zoom out", and grew):
   - **a life in squares:** two pinches and today is one small square among every day you'll have. The urgency, felt, not told.
   - **a day passing:** the life bar on my days pours in and the count turns by one, once a day. Time, gently.
   - **your days as paintings:** a month or a year of squares turned into a painting you can keep and share. Time, made beautiful.
   The zoom makes you feel time; the paintings make it beautiful. Paintings belong here, not as a fourth hero: they only exist because you painted, so they never compete with the brush. And a hero deserves a proper door: they shouldn't stay hidden.
3. **The question: "am I spending Today meaningfully?"** The voice of squared. Not how you feel, but what you did with your life today. **And squared speaking back** (nomi, Oct 30, 2026): once a day is painted, its heading answers in squared's own words, one line kept per day, chosen by what squared knows about it (today, a birthday, the early years, framed, far back, a torn day…). A dialog, not a form: it gives a sense of comfort. Every line is written and approved by hand (`docs` "squared: words for past days").

**The loop:** ask (the question) → paint (the brush) → see (your life). Every day comes down to it.

**The supporting cast:** moments (and their own colours), framing a day, out of my hands, tags. They serve the question's honesty; none is a hero, and together they're where squared could start to feel busy. Watch them.

## How it should feel

Minimal, super pleasant, easy. You feel drawn to it, not pushed. Calm, never techy.

## The test

Before adding anything, ask: which hero does this serve?

- The day points to the brush.
- The zoom points to your life.
- My days points to what you've painted: seeing your life, in squares, in time and in paintings.

If it serves none, it goes (stats, counts, extra rows). If it competes with a hero, it goes.

## Decided so far

- **The paint is gouache, dabbed:** soft round dabs with bristle dots, opaque, matte, settling into one flat coat. Not watercolour (colours would blur into each other). **Never change the brush** without nomi asking.
- **The day's word** ("worth it", "regret", "a blur") signs the lower right corner, like an artist's signature, in a deeper shade of its paint (dark, not white).
- **The zoom out's line is "You are here."** (debated Oct 27, 2026: over "This is today." and "Here, today."). Three words; the picture does the urgency, the words only put you in it. Never longer.
- **Never:** streaks, badges, scores, feeds, dashboards of numbers.

## Built

- **The paint dries** (lab row 6, A3): your textured strokes stay as painted, then soften into one smooth colour as they dry, and the word signs the corner with its sound. No gloss, no darker "wet" step (it read as a blink). The brush itself never changes.
- **A life fills in, every time** (lab row 7): the lived days fill in to today, a wave of grey even with nothing painted, then "You are here." Slower the first time; a touch skips it.
- **No counts in my days.**

- **A feature freeze until beta 1** (Oct 31, 2026): no new features until friends tell us which of the supporting cast they actually reach for. The one exception: giving the paintings their door.
