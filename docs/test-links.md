## my days' what-matters card: when it asks

- **No worth-it lines yet:** "what matters" / "What makes a day worth it to you?", a writing box (leaving it, or return, keeps your line). Kept: "added to what matters ✓", then the card becomes the next case. It stays until a line is written. Test it on any account with ?askfirst.
- **Worth-it lines written:** "you said this matters", one of your lines (a different one each visit); tap it for both lists.

(A week's and a first red day's questions, asked once each, were tried and cut, Nov 3, 2026: once-and-gone was too weak, and "add this to what matters" in the moment bubble already asks at the true moment.)
