# The big picture

_last updated Oct 25, 2026_

Where squared is, in the big steps. Many moving parts, one road. Each step has its own doc; this one only says where we are. Move the "you are here" marker as steps finish. The road's docs are numbered by their step (`2-beta-readiness.md` is step 2); a step with no doc of its own has no file, and two steps at the same time would be 3a and 3b. Reference docs (squared's heroes, brand story, design system, future ideas, test links) have no number: they're used at every step.

## The road

### 1 · I trust squared ← you are here

Not only the app: the story too. Ready means comfortable, not perfect. **A time box: about two weeks** (until early Nov). If it isn't there by then, write down what's missing and decide, rather than keep polishing.

- [ ] **The app:** a quiet week of just painting, no design changes; anything odd goes to Claude. (Used it 5 or 6 days so far, changing it daily.)
- [ ] **The crash log** is clean (`client_errors`, see beta readiness).
- [ ] **The brand story** feels true: "squared starts today", the line, the three colours (`docs/brand-story.md`).
- [ ] **Open questions** I can live with for now, decided or parked:
  - the past: lived, not blank (parked until testers, `docs/future-ideas.md`)
  - ~~onboarding step 3's question~~ (onboarding is gone, Oct 25, 2026: moments first, set up later from my days)

### 2 · squared is safe for other people's days

`docs/2-beta-readiness.md`, "Before beta": Supabase Pro backups, two-factor logins, domain auto-renew, the first backup download.

### 3 · squared on my iPhone

`docs/3-ios-todo.md`, steps 1 to 4: the Apple account, the native shell, sign-in (Apple and Google), the evening reminder. Then TestFlight, just me.

### 4 · The first circle

`docs/4-testflight-todo.md`: 10 to 20 people, two weeks, honest feedback. Watch what they do, especially with the past.

### 5 · Listen, then decide

Sort what testers said: fix now, try next, parked. Decide the big ones (the past). A second round if needed.

### 6 · The App Store

`docs/3-ios-todo.md`, steps 5 and 6: the store page, review, release by hand. Launch day: `docs/6-growth.md`.

### 7 · After launch

Private notes (end-to-end encryption) first (`docs/future-ideas.md`), then what real people ask for.

### 8 · Promote and support squared

Later, nothing to do yet: how squared keeps finding people after launch, and how people get help (questions, answers, App Store reviews). Its doc, `8-…`, is made when we get here; launch day itself is in `docs/6-growth.md`.

## Remember

- One step at a time. Design ideas wait in future ideas, they don't jump the road.
- The app is ready when I trust it, not when it's perfect.
- squared starts today. So do I.
