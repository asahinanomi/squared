# squared.today — Brand Story & Key Messaging

_The single source of truth for what squared says and why. Every screen pulls from here. v1 · Oct 18, 2026 · — nomi_

---

## 1. One line

squared is a daily conscience tracker. Each day you paint one square — one honest answer to **"did I spend today meaningfully?"** Zoom out, and the squares show the shape of your life.

## 2. The idea (the why)

- The **practice** is awareness — noticing your days *as you live them*.
- The **goal** is a meaningful day — present, and spent on what matters to you.
- The **enemy** is the day that slips past unnoticed. (The one that made this app exist.)

Rooted in Covey's urgent-vs-important: a day can be busy and still not matter. squared asks you to notice the difference, one day at a time.

## 3. The model (why there are exactly three colors)

Every day answers two hidden questions:
1. **Were you awake to it?** — present, or on autopilot
2. **Did it go to what matters?** — meaningful, or busywork

| | went to what matters | didn't |
|---|---|---|
| **you were there** | 🟩 mattered | 🟥 empty |
| **on autopilot** | — | ⬜ slipped |

🟩 and 🟥 are siblings — both days you *lived*. ⬜ is the outlier — the day you weren't there for.

## 4. The three colors — LOCKED

Use the **one-liner** wherever there's room (paint screen, definition page). Use the **short label** only in tight UI (pie legend, zoom views). Never invent new phrasings.

| color | short label | one-liner |
|---|---|---|
| 🟩 green | **mattered** | yes — I spent it on what matters. |
| 🟥 red | **empty** | no — I was busy, but none of it mattered. |
| ⬜ grey | **slipped** | I don't know — I wasn't paying attention. |

Voice rule: all three are **first person** (it's your own conscience, not the app grading you) and follow the skeleton **[yes / no / I don't know] — [honest clause].**

## 5. The daily question — LOCKED

> **did I spend today meaningfully?**

- First person. Always.
- The three colors are its three honest answers (yes / no / couldn't say).
- "meaningful" is squared's north-star word — it lives in the question and the definition page, never as a color label.

## 6. "Meaningful" — the definition (what meaningful means page)

> Not a good day or a bad one. Not what you got done.
> A day is meaningful when you were **aware** of it, and spent it on **what matters to you**.

Then the three answers (section 4).

## 7. Data states — SEPARATE vocabulary

These describe squares, not feelings. Keep them out of the three-color language so the palette stays clean.

| state | term | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| lived but not painted | **unpainted** | a day you lived but didn't log |
| not yet lived | **ahead** | squares still to come |

## 8. Voice principles

- First person, lowercase, plain-spoken. Honest, never preachy.
- No streaks. No scores. No judgment from the app — only your own.
- Warm, quiet, a little tender. It's a conscience, not a coach.
- Say the honest thing simply ("I wasn't paying attention") rather than dressing it up.
- Signed **— nomi** where a human voice appears (idea page).
