# Growth

_last updated Oct 25, 2026_

How people find squared. The short version: the store brings credibility, people bring people.

## What we believe

- **The App Store alone brings few people.** It makes squared feel real and easy to keep; people still come because someone showed them.
- **Depth before size.** 10 to 20 real users first (`docs/testflight-todo.md`), then more.
- **Don't wait.** Every week without users is a week of guessing.
- **Never pushy.** squared is calm; so is how it's shared.

## In order

1. **The first circle on TestFlight**: honest friends and a few strangers. See `docs/testflight-todo.md`.
2. **Where the people already are:**
   - "year in pixels" (bullet journaling: r/bulletjournal, r/journaling)
   - "your life in weeks" (Tim Urban) and *Four Thousand Weeks* readers: time, meaning, memento mori
3. **nomi's own paintings, shared:** a month or a year in colour on Instagram or Threads, with the line "The past is painted. Today is still yours." Quiet, beautiful, not selling.
4. **Store launch day:** Product Hunt, a post, and the testers who loved it saying so.
5. **The App Store page itself:** subtitle, screenshots and keywords in the brand story's voice (`docs/ios-todo.md`, step 5).

## Not doing

- Ads, paid installs, referral rewards: not squared's voice, and not needed yet.
- Streaks, badges, social feeds to "drive engagement": never techy, never a dashboard.
